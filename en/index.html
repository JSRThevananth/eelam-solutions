Questions before you start

What does the $1,000 include?

Everything listed under the package. Nothing is hidden. Taxes (GST and QST) are added where they apply.

When does the price change?

Only when you ask for something outside the package, such as extra pages, a larger print run or a monthly care plan. You'll get the price in writing before any extra work starts.

How long does a project take?

Two to three weeks for most packages, once we have your content. Printed materials ship about a week after you approve the design.

Do I own my website and files?

Yes. When the project is paid, the site, design files and app are yours.
